{ // 2. God, Devil and Demiurge - The Natural Order Thesis 
"Is there a Devil? A single spiritual entity which embodies evilness?" In the Christian tradition, the Devil is the enemy of mankind; it wants to destroy humanity and Gods creation. In this document, we dont deny the possibility, but we avoid considering this kind of phenomenon to be solely from one source of evil. We consider the transient world of spirits which affects the physical world to be composed of different evils, which could or not unite to pursue a single purpose, but often displaying rogue behavior and composed primarily of rogue human spirits, instead of foreign spiritual entities.

In this text, we use the concept of archetypes. They are concepts of spiritual behaviors which spirits could perform, but behaving as an archetype is not the same as being the archetype; they are roles, and they do not necessarily exist as described by this text. So when we say that a spirit embodies the archetype of a demon, it is not the same as saying that this spirit is a demon. We dont commit to asserting about intangible entities; we are only saying that their behavior fits a defined archetype.

"Natural Order and Evil." In this document, we consider that evil arises from the deviation of what we call the natural order. The natural order is the correct relationship between the component of order and the component of chaos. Order is responsible for maintaining stability and information, preserving properties to keep existence going, and chaos has a supportive role: to give sufficient instability to drive existences toward evolution. There are two kinds of evil: static evilness, which arises from the denial of the supportive role of chaos, making everything unchangeable and static, halting evolution; and the inversion of the natural order, which is the inversion of roles when chaos becomes too strong, denying its supportive role and promoting destructive instability.

The Devils evilness can be defined as the inversion of order; it wants the destruction of the world by making the chaos component of beings too strong. But there is another kind of evil, which doesnt want destruction; instead, it wants to preserve its status by halting the development of the world and keeping everything static, what we call the Demiurge evilness, or the Deceiver evilness. The Demiurge evilness is more subtle than the Devil evilness, but both represent a deviation from the natural order and so both are considered evil. In the transient spiritual world, both Demiurgic and Devilish influences are considered to afflict the physical world of higher spiritual beings (humans).

"Devil, Demiurge and Gods Archetype." We consider three kinds of spiritual behavior based on the natural order. The Devil is the archetype of inversion: everything that should have a supportive role is shifted to the main role, and what should be in the main role is denied or forced into a supportive role. As this inversion often manifests as increasing chaos, Devilish influence frequently leads to destruction. The Demiurge archetype is the absence of development, or stagnation, and is often displayed as the revelation of truth coupled with a demand for absolute faith and obedience. It is subtle because it reveals truth, yet it is fallacious (the whole from a part) and can be recognized because it is arrogant, has presence, selfishness, and ego. The Demiurge evilness leads to stagnation; the Demiurge is the archetype of the usurper of God. Finally, the Gods archetype is the archetype of silent help,giving inspirations, but never showing oneself. Their presence is at the same time undeniable yet inconclusive; one cannot distinguish luck from the influence of the Gods archetype. The Gods archetype is the archetype of creation itself, so its presence is undeniable, but not in a rational sense, as the world can be explained purely from a physical perspective if one denies that chaos can be tempered by spiritual force.

All spirits should pursue the Gods archetype to effectively reincarnate, because that is the only way higher spirits reincarnate. They should deny all specifics, the information that binds them to their old lives, and an effective way to do so is by doing good without expecting any reward or recognition. The Gods archetype represents the natural order between the spiritual world and the physical world, with the physical world playing the main role and the spiritual world the supportive role. The Demiurge archetype is the spiritual path that seeks to preserve what was achieved spiritually: good spirits that deviated from the Gods path, believing there is no God, and often trying to occupy that place, usurping the role. The Devil archetype is the archetype of destruction, spirits that want to destroy the world, demanding something else, often arising as a demand for change from Demiurgic control.  

"Undeniable, but at same time inconclusive." The soundness of atheism and agnosticism is a direct consequence of the possibility of God following His own archetype. Because if He enforced His presence, enforced His view of the world, and enforced direct control over how things should be, He would become a Demiurge and lead the world to stagnation. The Gods archetype is paradoxical in nature: it is undeniable, yet at the same time inconclusive. The concept of truth is related to Gods archetype, even if you achieved a powerfull theory, which possibly explain everything about the world, one cannot deny the faculty of doubt, the truth is true only if it can be challenged by any other subsequent theory and remain sound.

{ // 3. Spiritual Phenomena and Spiritual Energy 
"Spiritual Force Acts Transforming Chaos into Order." The spiritual world does not violate any physical laws; it only acts on the inner chaos of the physical world. A mentally ill person simply has more chaos, providing more ways for a spiritual being to act, but a healthy person can also be affected by spiritual force, because chaos is inherent to our physiology and to reality itself.

Coincidence is the exact, very nature of spiritual phenomena, so yes, but that is not a valid argument against it. Nevertheless, randomness is just a gate, which may or may not be tempered by a spiritual source, and the spiritual world cannot violate any physical law, including distribution laws.

The chaos in order transformation also explain the conciousness self spirituality, the will is the spirit performing this transformation inside our minds. That transformation also occurs when someone has a thought in his imagination, our spirit guides this transformation, so we act and effectively make choices, because we have a spirit performing this, instead of just been a priviledged watcher of ourselves.

The difference between the spiritual world and the physical world is one of magnitude: the physical world is simply a will that is too strong, which can only be tempered by a collection of spiritual forces.
