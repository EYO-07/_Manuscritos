{ // 1. Introduction 
"What is Natural Order Communism?" It is the communist theory based on Natural Order Dialectics instead of Hegelian/Materialistic Dialectics. Natural Order Dialectics is a conceptual model characterized by an asymmetric relationship between two entities associated with Order and Chaos. Order assumes the main role, preserving what has been achieved, while Chaos has a supportive role, preventing stagnation. Natural Order Dialectics is based on nature—specifically, the evolution that occurs in living beings, which is essentially conservative while allowing stable evolution through random mutation. 

Main % Support 
Order % Chaos 

"Is Natural Order just another conservative movement in disguise?" Natural Order theory does not dictate how much chaos should exist within the dialectical relationship. Humanity has a stronger chaotic component than other animals; we are more inventive, and we create and change things more quickly than natural evolution does. That is why humanity can shape reality according to its will. What Natural Order says is that too much chaos will lead to instability and destruction, so we always place chaos in a supportive role—change serves evolution, and not the contrary. How much chaos should humanity have? As much as it pleases, provided it is not at the expense of self-destruction and civilizational collapse. 

"So Natural Order is not revolutionary?" Placing chaos in the main role is placing mutation as more important than existence, which will logically lead to an aberration. If that is what being revolutionary means, then Natural Order and Natural Order Communism are indeed conservative. Nevertheless, Natural Order is revolutionary locally, within the context of the changes necessary for evolution, yet conservative, as it does not aim to disregard every other civilizational achievement. 

"But what changes does Natural Order Communism propose?" If it did not propose anything, merely denying chaos and revolution, it would indeed be a conservative, reactionary movement against true communism. So what does Natural Order Communism propose? Natural Order Communism focuses on the correct order between Labor and Liberty among humanity. People who deny the liberty of others merely to exploit their labor should not be tolerated; all people should have liberty, and their liberty should support the existence of society. Thus, labor occupies the main role—the order role—for everyone, and liberty occupies the supportive role. Liberty cannot be denied; instead, it should be maximized. The amount of liberty should be roughly proportional to the amount of contribution to society, whether as a direct labor contribution or as a contribution toward liberation from the necessity of labor. The main goal of the Natural Order Communism movement is the fight against oppression, the fight against the denial of individual liberty, the fight against labor exploitation, and the maximization of individual rights and liberty.

Labor % Liberty 

"What happens if the necessity of labor vanishes in a society?" When the necessity of labor ceases to exist, a true Natural Order Communist society will be achieved. In this scenario, the pursuit of creation, innovation, and ways to satisfy others desires will become the labor activity. In this way, labor conceptually never vanishes, even though the natural or social necessity of labor will cease to exist. Labor is the goal and purpose of human beings, in the sense that humans will use their liberty to create things and to innovate; thus, even without the necessity of labor, labor will still be pursued because it is in human nature.

"Catharsis. What happens to older communist theories?" All other communist theories are contaminated by an inversion of the Natural Order. Materialistic Dialectics is an inversion of Natural Order itself; it is the inversion between the Reality and Mind contexts in original Hegelian Dialectics. Contrapositive dialectics is natural to the human mind, as conflict should exist in ideas, never in reality within the context of human interactions. Conflict in reality is the regression of civilization. Materialistic Dialectics emphasizes dialectical conflict in reality, which could only be justified if oppression is so strong that the debate of ideas is impossible. Yet, the evolution of civilization should place conflict within the realm of ideas. The inversion of Natural Order becomes attractive to people with inverted values, which subsequently helps spread this corruption. To understand what should be done, one must first understand the three archetypes based on Natural Order Dialectics. In short, with inverted theories, one should simply flip the concepts to make them usable. "What is Dialectics?" Dialectics, in Natural Order Communism and in general dialectics, has two meanings that could at first be considered separately. First, there is Socratic Dialectics, which is the pursuit of knowledge through Socratic dialogue. Second, there is Dialectical Modeling, which is the creation of an abstract model, called a dialectical basis, and the projection of concepts onto this basis to understand them. Hegelian Dialectics is one dialectical basis; Natural Order is another dialectical basis.

"Dialectics and Communism." The concept of dialectics is essential to understanding the development of both communism and Natural Order Communism, as both share its use, though they employ different kinds of dialectics to reach their conclusions.  

"Dialectical Modeling Artifacts." The dialectical method is not deterministic; it is not imperative, and it cannot be used to perform deductive reasoning. Dialectical Modeling is a process for creating a model. It is fundamentally an inductive process, which means it can lead to artifacts—artificial insertions that can result in misrepresentations of reality. Hegelian Dialectics puts too much emphasis on conflict, so conflict and tension become the leading tone of communism, whereas Natural Order Dialectics emphasizes complementarity to achieve a purpose.

"Tension in Natural Order Communism." Conflict exists in Natural Order Communism, though not in the correct dialectical form; conflict will always be present whenever the Natural Order disposition is denied. There are two dispositions considered incorrect in Natural Order Dialectics: when the main role denies the supportive role (Order(Main) % *), meaning order denies chaos and leads to stagnation; and when chaos usurps the main role (Chaos(Support) X Order(Main)), meaning chaos leads to destruction. We call (Labor % Liberty) the projection of human purpose onto Natural Order Dialectics. Looking into (Labor % Liberty), we can identify two incorrect dispositions: (Labor % *), which means exploitation through the effective denial of individual liberty, and (Liberty X Labor), which is the conflict between usurpers and workers.

"Natural Order Dialectics vs. Hegelian Dialectics." Natural Order Dialectics considers stagnation an incorrect disposition, so in the correct Natural Order, the chaotic part must be present. Inner tension is the chaotic part, which is essential to Natural Order Dialectics and is responsible for continuous development. In this way, Natural Order Dialectics does not deny Hegelian dialectical inner tensions; rather, the model identifies that tension (the areas that can be improved) as inversion (conflict between supportive and main roles) and stagnation (the denial of the supportive role).

"Natural Order Duality of Evils." Evil, or incorrectness, is displayed in two forms in Natural Order Dialectics: Inversion (Support X Main) and Stagnation (Main % *). The Natural Order model guides development through the identification of these two problems that can arise in an asymmetric relationship. ... 

"Can Chaos occupy the main role?" Nevertheless, what defines which entity occupies the main or supportive role in Natural Order Dialectics is the objective within its proper context. For instance, in evolution, Order occupies the main role and Chaos is supportive. If the objective does not require stability—for example, if the objective is to generate a new idea—Chaos can occupy the main role. To generate a new idea, it may be necessary to make radical changes to surpass the current course of thought. In creativity, Chaos can occupy the main role, while Order is responsible for adjusting the idea, making it reasonable, and fitting it into reality. That inversion is actually what is supposed to happen in the relation between Mind and Reality: Mind is more chaotic by nature, so Chaos can occupy the main role, while reality and existence are conservative by nature, because in reality, things must work. In Natural Order Dialectics, the concepts of Main and Support induce and require the concept of purpose; something is Main for a goal, and Support for an objective.

{ // 3. Communist Statements through the Natural Order Optic 
"Exploitation and means of production." Original Marxist communism states that the capitalist exploits the surplus value of workers labor, which is the value of labor minus the wage given to the worker. However, Marxism fails to understand the intellectual labor accumulated through good decisions, good management, and the very creation of the technology necessary for the means of production to exist. Investment, technology, and management—all intellectual work—are accumulated in the private property of the means of production. The proletarian takeover of the means of production is not a fair claim, especially in a society where, to some extent, you can own a small shop or a small manufacturer if you manage to save resources. Natural Order Communism does not define exploitation in this manner; instead, it defines it conceptually, using Natural Order Dialectics (Labor % Liberty), where exploitation and oppression are the abuse of labor and the denial of liberty.
1. (Corruption) Political Oppression: the abusive extraction of labor and denial of liberty through taxes to support the political class, as well as political monopoly through political party filtering.
2. (Monopoly) Economic Oppression: the denial of the free market, where the economic establishment allies with the political establishment to impose bureaucratic barriers that prevent people from other classes from effectively or legally acquiring means of production.
3. (Dogmatism) Intellectual Oppression: the denial of free thinking, the denial of free speech, and the contamination of academic spaces and the education system to deny liberty of thought.

"Private Property in Natural Order Communism." The denial of private property in original Marxism is linked to the ownership of the means of production. Since the ownership of the means of production is not considered oppression or exploitation in Natural Order Communism, that premise is not required in a Natural Order Communist society. What should be pointed out is that a monopoly, or the effective monopolization of the means of production, should not exist in Natural Order Communism.

"Taxes in Natural Order Communism." Taxation by the political class over the labor class is one of the main sources of exploitation. Oddly, this surplus value stolen from both workers and owners of the means of production is, in general, completely ignored by contemporary communist and socialist movements. Taxes should be minimized or even eliminated in Natural Order Communism. One could say that instead of a moneyless, stateless, classless society, true communism should be a taxless society. 

"Is Natural Order Communism an Anarchist Movement?" Interaction between members of society can be free, but only if those members follow rules and laws. It is the sign of a utopian movement if it disregards any kind of enforcement. The third component of the relation between (Leader % Worker) is the Contract, defined according to Laws and enforced by a third party. Government arises from the necessity of that third component (not only for economic relations) and is considered necessary for stability. Nevertheless, the third party should be considered a service like any other; as a service, it should be paid for directly within the contract rather than funded through various forms of taxation, and its price should be ruled by the free market. Thus, the government should not monopolize the contract market and should not act directly in any interaction; instead, it should be placed in a higher position to resolve disputes. Political exploitation disguises itself in obscurity and various forms of mandatory taxes; from a Natural Order Communism perspective, government services should be paid for voluntarily and directly per service, not supported by mandatory taxes.

"Ownership of means of production and Wage-Workers." Instead of defining classes by the ownership of the means of production, Natural Order Communism defines this relation through the concept of economic leadership. A leader is a role for someone who has greater intellectual and decision-making responsibility, and a subordinate is a role more directly related to performing tasks, involving more direct labor (human time and energy consumption). The relation (Leader % Subordinate) is the key to Natural Order Communism adopting the free market as a core principle. Leadership is scarce by nature, making it a competitive role; in a free market, economic leadership is scarce and can therefore be optimized through competition under free-market rules. To keep this relation fair, there must be no effective barriers preventing someone from transitioning from a subordinate to a leadership position. An economic leader should have rights against unfair takeovers, and a subordinate should have the right to be paid for their work. In this scheme, there is no surplus exploitation, because we do not deny the intellectual work done by the leader. Exploitation takes place when the free market fails to optimize the leadership position, making subordination mandatory under effectively unfair conditions for those who subordinate themselves. The absence of a free market is the root cause of exploitation, because subordination under unfair conditions becomes mandatory for the working class.

"Money, Labor, and Liberty." The moneyless property of communism is meaningless in Natural Order Communism. There will be money as a practical valuation for the exchange between labor and liberty. Money or currency is a quantifiable object or entity that can effectively map the amount of labor and liberty. Liberty and labor are analogous to energy in physical systems: money is the accumulated energy of the market system, while labor is the energy in use. With money, there will be prices, and prices store information about the demand for a service or good in the free market. 

"Establishment and Bourgeoisie." The Bourgeoisie, as the current owners of the means of production, are not the main challenge to the working class in Natural Order Communism. The actual threat is the Establishment, which is defined as an effective monopoly of the system composed of current economic and political leaders. The Establishment is always tempted to exploit systemic failures to gain advantages or even suppress the free market. The Marxist Bourgeoisie is just one possible Establishment that can arise in the context of a non-free market. 

"Etymology of communism." Yet, we must point out that the original concept of communism is related to the word "common," signifying social ownership of the means of production. In Natural Order Communism, social ownership is not the main goal, so it may seem like a misuse of the term. For Natural Order Communism, the fight against oppression is the main goal. A Natural Order Communist society will be one in which everyone can interchange between roles (classes) more easily, so the boundaries between classes become weaker. In this scenario, a person could work, accumulate resources, buy ownership of the means of production for some activity, and later sell that ownership to someone else, all within a single lifetime. The roles of economic leadership and subordination will be "common" in the sense that they are not exclusive; from a dynamic perspective, society will be effectively classless.
